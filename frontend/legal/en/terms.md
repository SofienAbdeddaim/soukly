## Purpose of the service {#purpose}

Soukly is a classified ads site for sales and services in Tunisia. It lets you post listings, browse them and contact their authors.

## Account {#account}

Posting a listing requires an account verified by email. The account information must be accurate, and the password stays personal.

## Posting listings {#listings}

Every listing follows the [posting rules](/en/legal/posting-rules). A listing stays online for 60 days, then can be put back online from "My listings".

## Contact between members {#contact}

A seller's phone number is only shown to signed-in members. Exchanges and transactions take place directly between members.

## Sharing on social networks {#social-sharing}

If you link your Facebook or Instagram accounts, Soukly can publish your listings there on your behalf. See the [privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy).
