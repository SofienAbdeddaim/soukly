## Data we collect {#data-collected}

- Your account: email address, display name, phone number, preferred language and password (never stored in clear text).
- Your listings: texts, price, location and photos.
- The numbers you reveal on other members' listings (listing and date), to limit abuse.
- If you link Facebook or Instagram: the information needed to publish on your Page or account.

## Purposes {#purposes}

This data is used to run your account, publish your listings, let buyers contact you, send you the emails of the service and, if you choose to, share your listings on your social networks.

## Retention periods {#retention}

- A deleted listing disappears at once; its data and photos are erased for good 30 days later, along with the list of numbers revealed for that listing.
- A deleted account disappears at once; its data is erased for good 30 days later.
- Database backups are kept for 7 days.
- [Other periods to be completed after legal review.]

## Sharing with Facebook and Instagram {#social-networks}

Nothing is published on your social networks without your consent. If you link your accounts, Soukly publishes the text and photos of your listings there, without your phone number or email address. You can unlink your accounts at any time.

## Cookies and local storage {#cookies}

Soukly only uses one technical cookie, needed to stay signed in. Your browser also remembers your language, your governorate and the draft of your listing. No audience measurement or advertising tool is used.
