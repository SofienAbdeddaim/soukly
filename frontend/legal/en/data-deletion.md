## Delete your account {#delete-account}

1. Sign in and open [My profile](/en/account/profile).
2. Choose "Delete my account" and confirm with your password.
3. Your listings disappear at once; your data is erased for good 30 days later.

## Posts on Facebook and Instagram {#social-posts}

The posts Soukly published on your Facebook Page or Instagram account are removed as far as possible. You can also delete them yourself from Facebook or Instagram.

## Without access to your account {#no-access}

If you can no longer access your account, write to {{contactEmail}} from the account's email address.
