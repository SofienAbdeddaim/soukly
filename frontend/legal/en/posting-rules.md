## Good practices {#good-practices}

- One listing for a single item or a single service.
- Real photos of what you offer.
- A clear title and description, and an honest price.
- The right category and the right location.

## Forbidden items and services {#forbidden}

Indicative list, to be confirmed:

- weapons, ammunition and explosives;
- medicines and narcotics;
- counterfeits and stolen goods;
- animals of protected species;
- adult content.
